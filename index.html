About Neura

AI-powered learning, built for Web3.

Neura combines personalized AI lessons with token incentives. Instead of paying to learn, users earn NEURA while building real skills – from languages and coding to finance and Web3.

Problem

Learning is broken.

Online education is noisy, unstructured, and unmotivating. Platforms capture value, while learners get no tangible rewards for the time they invest.

  • Low engagement & retention
  • No real incentives
  • One-size-fits-all content
Solution

Learn-to-Earn with AI.

Neura turns every lesson into an on-chain reward opportunity. The more you learn, the more NEURA you earn – and the smarter the AI becomes with community feedback.

  • AI-generated micro-lessons
  • Rewarding quizzes & streaks
  • Community-trained AI model
How It Works

Three steps to start earning by learning.

Step 1

Connect wallet & choose a path.

Connect your Solana wallet, pick a skill path (e.g. English A1, Solana 101, Python Basics) and get personalized learning recommendations.

Step 2

Complete AI lessons & quizzes.

Neura generates short lessons and interactive quizzes tailored to your level. Sessions take 1–4 minutes and fit into your daily routine.

Step 3

Earn NEURA for progress.

Every completed task, streak, and path milestone earns NEURA rewards. Future features will let you redeem tokens for premium AI tools and certifications.

Community

Help train the AI.

Vote on answer quality, flag issues, and suggest new paths. The community helps train Neura’s AI and is rewarded for meaningful contributions.
